Our story

Meet the Kingdom in the Sky.

A country shaped by mountains, community and a strong sense of identity.

More than a destination

A country with its own rhythm.

Lesotho is a mountainous country completely surrounded by South Africa. Its high-altitude landscapes, villages and traditions give visitors a chance to experience a different pace of travel.

The Basotho people have a rich cultural heritage. The traditional Basotho blanket and mokorotlo hat are familiar symbols, while music, storytelling, food and community life remain important parts of everyday culture.

Visitors can help protect what makes a place special by respecting local customs, asking before taking photographs of people and leaving natural areas as they found them.

A taste of home

Food brings people together.

Traditional food and hospitality

Food is an important part of sharing and welcoming in Basotho communities. Visitors may encounter familiar staples and locally prepared meals during their travels.

When trying local food, take time to learn about the ingredients and the traditions behind each dish.

Travel thoughtfully

Small choices matter.

01

Respect local life

Be polite, learn a few local greetings and ask permission before photographing people or private spaces.

02

Support local businesses

Choose local guides, makers and places to eat when possible. Your visit can help local communities.

03

Leave no trace

Please do not litter and respect the natural environment so that others can enjoy it too.
